# Engineering Simulation & Research Portfolio

Welcome. I am Joseph Quansah, a mechanical engineer with developing experience in computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and finite element analysis (FEA). My previous work centers on thermo-fluid systems, industrial ventilation optimization, and structural-thermal analysis.

## Technical Expertise

* **Computational Fluid Dynamics:** ANSYS Fluent, flow modeling, thermal distribution analysis.
* **Finite Element Analysis & CAD:** SolidWorks (Simulation, static structural analysis, thermal stress evaluation).
* **Tools & Development:** COMSOL Multiphysics, Python (scientific computing).

## Research Projects

### 1. Industrial Building Floor Thermal Comfort Analysis (CFD)
* **Objective:** Investigated indoor environmental conditions and airflow patterns using a 3D CFD model in ANSYS Fluent.
* **Scope:** Evaluated velocity profiles and temperature distributions across single-boiler and double-boiler configurations to identify thermal hotspots and inform ventilation requirements.
* **Documentation:** Full methodology and findings are available in [CFD_Analysis.pdf](CFD_Analysis.pdf). Simulation visualization: `cfd-airflow.mp4`.

### 2. Structural and Thermal Analysis of Fire-Tube Boiler Designs (FEA)
* **Objective:** Evaluated the structural and thermal performance of fire-tube boilers under operational loads.
* **Scope:** Performed static FEA in SolidWorks comparing flat-end and dished-end plate configurations under internal pressure and thermal gradients, quantifying von Mises stress concentrations and deflections.
* **Documentation:** Detailed study report available in [FEA_Analysis.pdf](FEA_Analysis.pdf).
