About & Background

I am a mechanical engineer with developing experience in computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and finite element analysis (FEA). My work centers on thermo-fluid systems, industrial ventilation optimization, and the structural-thermal behavior of industrial thermal equipment.

My core research objective is applying high-fidelity numerical modeling and multiphysics simulations to analyze fluid-structure interactions and optimize thermal system efficiency.

Research Projects

1. Industrial Building Floor Thermal Comfort Analysis (CFD)

Objective & Scope: Investigated indoor environmental conditions and airflow behavior utilizing advanced 3D computational fluid dynamics modeling inside ANSYS Fluent. Evaluated internal velocity profiles and temperature distributions across single-boiler and double-boiler operational setups to isolate thermal hotspots and optimize ventilation performance.

View Full CFD Report (PDF)

2. Structural and Thermal Analysis of Fire-Tube Boiler Designs (FEA)

Objective & Scope: Performed comprehensive structural and thermal finite element evaluation on fire-tube boilers subjected to severe operational loads. Conducted static FEA simulations via SolidWorks contrasting flat-end versus dished-end geometries under 10 bar internal pressure and severe thermal gradients, quantifying stress concentrations and maximum plate deflections.
