Bio

I am a mechanical engineer with developing experience in computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and finite element analysis (FEA). My work centers on thermo-fluid systems, industrial ventilation optimization, and the structural-thermal behavior of thermal equipment.

My research goal is to apply high-fidelity numerical modeling and multiphysics simulation to improve thermal efficiency and structural reliability in complex engineering systems.

Selected Projects

Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)

Industrial Building Floor Thermal Comfort Analysis

Investigated indoor environmental conditions and airflow patterns using a 3D CFD model in ANSYS Fluent. Evaluated velocity profiles and temperature distributions across single-boiler and double-boiler configurations to identify thermal hotspots and inform ventilation requirements.

Finite Element Analysis (FEA) & CAD

Structural and Thermal Analysis of Fire-Tube Boiler Designs

Evaluated the structural and thermal performance of fire-tube boilers under operational loads. Performed static FEA in SolidWorks comparing flat-end and dished-end plate configurations under internal pressure and thermal gradients, quantifying von Mises stress concentrations and deflections.
