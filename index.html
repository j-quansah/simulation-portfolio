Bio

Joseph Quansah is a mechanical engineering graduate from Accra Technical University (B.Tech & HND) with a focused background in computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and finite element analysis (FEA). His academic research includes thesis-level CFD simulations for industrial ventilation optimization and FEA-based thermal-structural analysis of industrial boilers. He is currently expanding his computational toolkit into acoustic multiphysics modeling and foundational scientific programming, and is seeking M.S. and Ph.D. opportunities in thermo-fluid and multiphysics simulation research.

Projects

1. Industrial Ventilation CFD Analysis

B.Tech Thesis Project (Accra Technical University): Developed steady-state CFD simulations using ANSYS Fluent to evaluate airflow distribution, heat transfer rates, and ventilation effectiveness inside industrial facilities, providing design modifications to improve indoor thermal conditions.

2. Boiler Thermal-Structural FEA

HND Thesis Project (Accra Technical University): Conducted coupled thermal and structural finite element analysis (FEA) in SolidWorks on industrial boiler components under operating thermal loads to identify stress concentrations and evaluate structural integrity.
