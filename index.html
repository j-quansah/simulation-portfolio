Projects

1. Industrial Building Floor Thermal Comfort Analysis Using CFD

B.Tech Thesis Project (Accra Technical University): Conducted on-site environmental measurements (temperature & humidity) and developed a 3D CFD model in ANSYS Fluent to evaluate indoor airflow patterns, velocity fields, and temperature distributions across single-boiler and double-boiler operating scenarios. Results identified critical thermal hotspots near windows and central zones, guiding optimized ventilation requirements.

View CFD Thesis Report (PDF)

2. Structural and Thermal Analysis of Two Types of Fire-Tube Boiler Designs

HND Thesis Project (Accra Technical University): Performed static structural and thermal FEA simulations in SolidWorks on 1023 Carbon Steel and Gray Cast Iron fire-tube boilers comparing flat-end vs. dished-end plate configurations. The study evaluated von Mises stress distributions, thermal gradients, and displacements under 10 bar internal pressure and 100°C thermal loads, proving that dished-end plates significantly reduce stress concentrations and deflections.
