Research Background

I am a mechanical engineer with developing experience in computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and finite element analysis (FEA). My primary focus centers on thermo-fluid systems, industrial ventilation optimization, and the rigorous structural-thermal evaluation of heavy thermal equipment.

My overarching research goal is to bridge numerical modeling with high-fidelity multiphysics simulation to drive advancements in thermal efficiency and system reliability.

Simulations & Research Projects

Industrial Building Floor Thermal Comfort Analysis (CFD)

Investigated complex indoor environmental conditions and localized airflow behaviors utilizing a 3D CFD model developed in ANSYS Fluent. Evaluated high-resolution velocity contours and temperature distributions across single-boiler and double-boiler operational arrangements to isolate thermal hotspots and optimize ventilation layouts.

View Technical CFD Report (PDF)

Structural and Thermal Analysis of Fire-Tube Boiler Designs (FEA)

Performed rigorous structural and thermal finite element evaluation on fire-tube boilers under intense operational constraints. Conducted static FEA simulations via SolidWorks contrasting flat-end versus dished-end geometries under 10 bar internal pressure fields and severe thermal gradients, quantifying stress concentrations and deflection thresholds.
